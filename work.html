Carrier example

An office-and-driver system for a regional hauling operation, shown with sample data.

Part of Transportation O.S., built to run a hauling operation's office and its trucks in one place.

Driver hiring

Drivers apply from their phone in short steps they can stop and finish later. The office moves each one through screening, interview and file checks on one board.

Dispatch, driver app & e-tickets

Dispatch sees trucks and jobs. The driver gets the job on their phone with the address and steps in order. At the end the foreman can sign on the driver's screen; that signed ticket is what an invoice can be built from.

Invoicing

Signed tickets can turn into invoices without retyping. The customer's page shows the balance and a PDF. Steps that send money or a message can wait for a second person to approve them first.

A sample-data preview of a dealer website and leads board.
